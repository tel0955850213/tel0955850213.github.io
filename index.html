Industrial Gauge Reader

An inspection robot prototype sometimes read the wrong end of an analog gauge needle.

The fix separates finding the needle’s axis from choosing its tip. A calibrated ring provides evidence for which end to trust. When that evidence is weak, the reader can withhold the value.

MindTheStep

A LiDAR iPhone prototype that warns about steps and curbs before a fall, not after one.

The detector turns ARKit depth into gravity-aligned ground samples, rejects walls and clutter, and confirms a hazard across multiple frames before triggering haptics and speech. Four field tests produced four concrete fixes, each backed by a regression test.

CivicRadar

An iPhone app that tracks official legal and policy changes without confusing a proposal with an enacted rule.

The pipeline keeps legal effect separate from political outcome, links every claim to an official source, and quarantines conflicts or missing dates. Clients verify schema, counts, and SHA-256 digests before accepting a full or delta update.

Failure model

Karteria

A deliberate-practice app where progress is tied to observable evidence, not likes or an AI score.

Private practice stays on the device. A checkpoint is verified only when two of three calibrated peers independently confirm all required criteria. Anonymous review and trusted review remain separate systems, with different rules for minors.

Trust boundary

On-device AI can flag missing evidence before submission. It cannot create, change, or replace a peer vote.

How I work

Evidence before confidence.

  1. 01

    Make uncertainty visible

    Unknown, stale, and unverified are product states. I do not collapse them into a confident answer.

  2. 02

    Turn field failures into tests

    A chair that looked like a stair became a geometry rule and a regression case, not a line in a retrospective.

  3. 03

    Design for the free path to stop

    My hosted projects use hard free-tier limits. When a quota runs out, the service fails closed instead of creating a charge.

  4. 04

    Keep the decision inspectable

    Rules, source links, test fixtures, and data versions stay close to the feature they support.
